Qualification · what a lead must tell us

The owner decides which answers a lead needs before a broker takes over, and the conditions, like an IF node. Rania asks the next missing required question, one at a time, and keeps each answer with the customer's own words. Code, not the AI, applies the conditions, so the same answers always get the same verdict.

Only the owner can change the questions and conditions. You are viewing them.

After qualifying · recommendations

Qualified → Rania sends the best-fitting properties → the customer picks one → 🔥 the broker gets a "hot lead, ready to close" call card with the property and the customer's words.

The front door

A client who says they're only looking gets a warm reply and is not chased: their lead moves to 🌱 Nurture. Rania still answers them, and when they show they're ready (a budget, a viewing, a call) the lead comes back and the broker is told. "Can someone call me?" always reaches the broker.

When a client goes quiet

Rania follows up only when it helps: a new listing that fits, the one question still open, or the door left open. Never in quiet hours, never after "stop", and at most as many as you set. A reply starts the count again.
