AiSensy

Connect the AiSensy project your WhatsApp number lives on. Rania and your team then send and receive through it, and your approved templates appear in the Inbox.

Webhook for incoming messages

Save the project first; a private webhook address is created for you.
  1. In AiSensy, open Manage → Webhooks and add a webhook.
  2. Paste the address above and choose the message.created topic.
  3. Send a WhatsApp message to your number: it appears in the Inbox and Rania answers.

Instagram

An Instagram professional account connected to your Meta app. The studio reads your posts and their insights, studies competitors' public posts (business discovery), publishes approved reels, and passes comments to Rania.

Property portals

Your own listings and enquiries, through the access your brokerage already has. Diwan reads the same listing feed you give Property Finder, Bayut and Dubizzle, so it only ever offers what you advertise; nothing is scraped. A listing without a permit number is kept and never offered.

Enquiry webhooks

Give each portal (or your lead connector) its address and a shared secret. Each enquiry must be signed with it (HMAC-SHA256 in X-Signature), or carry it as ?token=. Without a secret, the address refuses everything.

Sales team

New leads go to the listing's agent, otherwise round-robin among brokers on shift. Calls are due in 15 minutes, replies in 30; untouched after an hour, a lead goes to the Pool. Notices show in the Inbox, and on each broker's own WhatsApp when you name an approved AiSensy template with one variable.

Deal kinds · sales desks

The kinds of deal your sales team works (like Ready sales, Off-plan, Leasing), each with the customers it serves, the listings it covers and its brokers. When a lead qualifies, the first kind whose rule matches is its kind: Rania recommends only that kind's listings, and the lead goes to one of its brokers (the listing's agent first). Your CRM's pipelines can fill this list too.

Only the owner changes the deal kinds. You are viewing them.

HeyGen · AI presenter

Lets the studio turn an approved script into a presenter video. Use only an avatar and voice of a person who agreed to it; every video is started by a person who confirms that.

Zoom · links for video calls
