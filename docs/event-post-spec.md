# Event posts: from your photos to a finished post

Session 1 (Marketing). Asked for by the product owner on 31 Oct 2026. Approved rule: AI-made graphics only, never a made-up
photo of something real.

## What a person does

1. In **Something just happened**, type what happened and the facts, and add photos.
2. Diwan says which format it suggests and why. Change it if you disagree.
3. Press **Write this post**: three options, each showing which photo carries which words.
4. Pick one. Images and the caption are made, the checks run, and it waits for publishing approval.

## What Diwan does

| Step | What | Paid? |
|---|---|---|
| Read the photos | Code measures size, sharpness and light. Muse Spark says what is in each photo, how strong it is and whether people are in it | About a cent |
| Search once | One web search on this event, with cited pages only | $0.07 to $0.70, once per moment |
| Choose the format | Rules below; the reason is shown | Free |
| Write | The three options, from the facts and the photos that exist | About a cent |
| Make the images | Crop to 1080 by 1350, the words on a band, the brand's name | Free |
| Graphic, when there are no photos | Words on a background; the background may be made by Muse Image | About $0.04 |

## How the format is chosen

| What exists | Format |
|---|---|
| A video clip | Reel |
| 4 or more usable photos | Carousel, or a reel from the photos when our own reels clearly beat our other posts |
| 1 to 3 usable photos | Single image |
| No photos | Graphic |

"Clearly beat": at least 3 of each, and reels average 1.5 times the others. These are starting rules, not measured results,
until the account has that history. A blurred, dark or very small photo is not counted as usable and the screen says so.

## Rules that always hold

- Real photos stay real: crop, words and a dark band only.
- Muse Image makes abstract backgrounds only: no people, buildings, products or text. Words are drawn by code, so they are
  spelled right.
- Photos with people are flagged on the publishing card so a person checks those people agreed.
- Words come from the facts given. The same review runs as for scripts.
- A person approves, and publishing stays off until the public address is set.

## Checklist (definition of done)

1. A moment takes photos, at creation or later, and the Run tab shows how many are usable.
2. Each of the four formats is chosen by the rules above, with the reason shown, and a person can change it.
3. A carousel shows each slide's photo and words before it is picked.
4. Approving makes real 1080 by 1350 images, the caption, the checks and a publishing request.
5. The publishing request names the format; approving says what is missing, or posts an image or a carousel.
6. The reel route still works: a clip goes through editing, and photos can become a reel with the computer's voice, on Windows too.
7. Without a model every step still works, labelled so: photos measured only, plain graphic, template words.
8. Errors: a file that is not a picture, a broken picture, more than 10 slides, a moment with no photos.
