# Marketing: research notes behind the upgrades

Gathered 2 November 2026 for the Marketing real-user test. Most sources are marketing blogs and tool vendors, not the
platforms' own help pages; treat sizes and lengths as current practice, and check the platform's help centre before a
large campaign. Numbers that disagree across sources are shown as a range.

## Post shapes per platform

| Platform | Picture | Video | Caption | Hashtags |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | 1080×1350 (4:5) preferred; 1080×1080 square | Reels 1080×1920 (9:16) | Short: the first two lines decide; about 140–150 characters reads best, 2,200 is the limit | 3–10 specific ones (sources vary) |
| Facebook feed | 1080×1350 or 1080×1080 display cleanly; links share at 1200×630 (1.91:1) | 9:16 or 4:5 | Can be longer, a link is allowed | 0–3 |
| LinkedIn | 1080×1350 or 1080×1080; links at 1200×627 (1.91:1) | 4:5 or 16:9 | Longer, insight-led (200–400 words do best); the feed cuts at about 210 characters, so the opening line must earn "see more"; one idea per line | 3–5 at the end |
| TikTok, YouTube Shorts | n/a | 1080×1920 (9:16) | A title (Shorts up to 100 characters) | 3–6 / 0–3 |

**One size that travels:** 1080×1350 works on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. The text should be adapted per
platform, never copied across.

## What makes a property reel work
- The first 1.5 to 3 seconds decide whether it is watched. Watch time is the main ranking signal.
- Reels start muted, so the first frame and the words on screen are the hook: test it with the sound off.
- Hooks that work: a bold claim, a curiosity gap, or a specific price or place, e.g. "This AED 3.1M Yas Acres villa has
  a garden on two sides — let me show you".
- Start talking at once (no pause at the start). Tight cuts every 2–3 seconds for a room-by-room tour.
- Authentic beats polished. A person to camera still works when it is edited tightly.
- Length advice conflicts (under 20 seconds to 60 seconds), so measure our own results.

## UAE occasions
- **Eid al Adha 2027:** expected Arafah Day on Saturday 15 May, then Eid on 16 to 18 May (a four-day public holiday,
  15 to 18 May). The date is confirmed by moon sighting, announced in late April or early May.
- The standard greeting is "Eid Mubarak", welcome from anyone. Tone is chosen for the audience.
- Arabic posts, and posts that reflect the festival's own meaning (Hajj, family, giving), are recommended for the region.
- **Our rule:** a greeting is a greeting. No price, no listing, no offer in the Eid post; a sales post can come after
  the holiday.
- Other dates for a calendar: UAE National Day (2 December), Commemoration Day (30 November), Ramadan and Eid al Fitr
  (moon-dependent), New Year.
