# Marketing: real-user test

Run in a real Chrome window on a copy of the GitHub test data (`data/try.db`), with publishing off and WhatsApp in sandbox. Each case has its pass rule written before it ran, two verdicts (it works; it makes sense for a brokerage marketer), a blind 1–5 score from a fixed judge ("would a professional Dubai brokerage publish this?") where something was made, and its evidence.

Model spend for the whole test: $11.15.

## What changed since the first run (2 November 2026)

Each fix below came from a case in this test; the tables further down keep every run's result, so a step listed under
"What a person still had to do" may come from a run before its fix.

| Found in the test | Now |
|---|---|
| Today's planned post had no scripts until someone pressed a button | A plan that includes today writes today's scripts straight away; each planned day is written that morning |
| The Marketing lead couldn't say today's post or its time, used asterisks and internal ids | It answers from the plan, the schedule and the posting hour, in plain text |
| Eid posts went out on the wrong day, sold a villa, had no greeting or logo | Occasions come from a calendar researched for the business's country (sources shown, Setup → Our occasions), are offered 30 days ahead with one yes, go out on their own day, greet without selling, and carry the logo and the Arabic greeting |
| A greeting card used a listing photo and the script's line as its headline | A greeting is a designed card by default, headed by the occasion's own greeting; the person can still choose their own team or office photos |
| Ask for change on a picture only reached the background | The request is worked out into what to change, the picture is made again without a new script round, and the Director checks before and after; the card shows both |
| Competitors' occasion posts were never looked at | Their posts around last year's day are read; the best ones brief the card, and the Director compares ours with theirs |
| A listing could only be a reel | The format comes from the listing's selling point, its photos and what wins in the trade (competitors' winners by format); a carousel or a single photo is made from the listing's own photos |
| The caption was a template: "Say BRAND", a Dubai hashtag on an Abu Dhabi villa, a 36-character hashtag | The Content writer writes it, held to the listing's facts, the permit and the code word; hashtags are checked; the code word is the listing's most distinctive word |
| The writer never saw size, bathrooms or handover | It gets every field, features read from the listing's own page (quoted) and area facts from a search (sourced) |
| Plan days couldn't be moved, and the posting days couldn't be chosen | Reschedule for planned and approved posts; Setup → Rhythm takes the posting days |
| The posting hour was an average over our own few posts | Our own results, else when our followers are online, else when competitors' winners went out, else the evening, and the card says which |
| Competitor posts from accounts no longer followed fed the ideas | Only saved competitors' posts are on the research board |

**Still weak, said plainly:** scripts score about 2 to 3 out of 5 from the blind judge. They are now factually right and
nothing is invented, but they still read as a list of features; the writer model follows the facts rules and not the
style rules. The free video (from photos) reached the approval card once and scored 2/5.
