# Marketing: the whole journey, from research to publishing

What a person does, what the agents do, how a script is built from your information, how approval and changes work, and
when a post goes out. Written from the code on 31 Oct 2026. For the owner, the Marketing manager and anyone new to the
Studio.

**Marks:** ✅ works today · 🔑 built, needs something connected · ❌ not built.

## 1. The user journey: what a person does

| Step | Who | What they do | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| Set up | Marketing manager | Setup tab: business, brand, competitors, who approves | ✅ |
| Teach the company | Marketing manager | Confirm company facts in Knowledge; the website can propose them | ✅ |
| See what is next | Marketing manager | Run tab, **Your next post**: the stage, the post's job and why | ✅ |
| Something happened | Marketing manager | **Something just happened**: the facts and photos | ✅ |
| Start | Marketing manager | **Write this post**, or run any listing, or turn on the daily automatic run | ✅ |
| Pick | The person named in Setup | **Use this one**, **Ask for change**, or **Send all back** | ✅ |
| Film | The broker | Gets a film list as a task, films, uploads the take | ✅ |

Only the person named in Setup, or the owner, can pick or approve. Until someone is named, anyone in Marketing can.

## 3. How a script is built from your information

The writer gets these, and only these:

| Goes in | From | Enforced how |
|---|---|---|
| The job of this post | The journey | An instruction |
| The facts | The listing, product, topic or moment | Any price not on the listing is rejected |
| Company facts | Confirmed Knowledge cards, at most 6, the most relevant first | A number claimed about us that no source shows is sent back |
| Brand voice | Setup: who we are, audience, voice, words we use | An instruction; a person judges it when picking |
| Words and claims we never use | Setup | Checked in code: the script goes back |
| Lessons | What you taught the Script writer | "Never" lessons are checked in code |
| Style notes | Learned from your picks and changes | An instruction |
| Why a competitor's post worked | Its topic, hook, who is on screen | Never their words, numbers, awards or partners |
| The photos | What is in each | Words go on the photo that shows them |

Every script must also pass: hook of 12 words or fewer, 5 to 8 lines, no promised returns, no placeholders, no repeated
line, a closing invitation, and not too close to a script already used. A post about the brand, a topic or a moment also
goes back if it tells a personal memory or belief the facts do not show ("I thought...", "Most buyers think...").

**How competitors shape a script.** A competitor's reason for winning reaches a script only when it is proven: at least 3 clear
winners sharing the same thing. With one competitor that is often not reached, and the script then leans on trends and
your facts, and says so. Four or five competitors give it enough to work with.

## 4. Which format, and who makes it

| What exists | Format | What happens after the pick |
|---|---|---|
| A video clip | Reel | Editing starts when footage arrives |
| 4 or more usable photos | Carousel | Images made straight away; nothing to film |
| 1 to 3 usable photos | Single image | The same |
| No photos | Graphic | Words on a plain or AI-made abstract background |
| A listing or topic with no event | Reel | Film it, free video, or AI presenter |

A person can change the format. Real photos are only cropped and given words on a band; AI-made images are abstract
backgrounds only. If our own reels clearly beat our other posts, photos can become a reel.

## 5. Captions

There are two kinds, and they are different things:

- **On the video:** burned in, timed to what was said. If the sound can't be transcribed, the script's lines are spread over
  the video, and it says so. A carousel or image has its words on the picture instead.
- **Under the post:** written by the Content writer, with the permit number when one is needed, the sign-off and the hashtags.

## 6. Approval: two gates

**Gate 1, the script.** Before anything costs time or money. **Gate 2, the finished post.** Before anyone outside sees it.
Nothing passes either gate without a person.

## 7. When a change is asked, where it goes

| Asked | When | Goes to | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Make the hook shorter" | Picking a script | Script writer, that script only | Checked, then offered again |
| "None of these" | Picking a script | Script writer | New script, checked, offered again |
| "Shorter caption", "fewer hashtags" | Finished post | Content writer | Same checks, back to the approver |
| "Different first photo", "change the words" | Finished post | Script writer | A new pick, new images, back to the approver |
| "The lighting is bad, film it again" | Finished reel | The broker, as a task | Editing runs on the new take |
| A failed check | Any time | The agent who made the work | Twice at most, then a person |
| A permit hold | The last gate | Whoever caused it | Checked again |
| **Stop this post** | Finished post | Nobody | It ends there: see below |

**What Stop does.** Everything made for that post leaves the Run tab and can't be posted: script, film list, video or images, caption and AI frames. The broker's film task is withdrawn, its day comes off the plan and stays empty, and it is listed under Came off. Real photos stay. Cancel on the "why" question stops nothing. It doesn't come back by itself for 30 days, except one of the first nine posts, which is still to do and comes back as a new version. Running it again starts clean, and the reason goes to the learner.

**A yes that can't happen changes nothing.** With publishing off, or a time already past, Approve and Post now say what is missing and the post keeps waiting. **Cancel it** puts a scheduled post back to waiting for a decision.

**Your turn shows only the post you have open.** Other posts waiting are links at the bottom. A background refresh leaves what you typed or chose alone unless there is something new to decide.

## 8. When it goes out

- **Approve for this time:** holds the post until that time. A person can change the time on the card. 🔑
- **At the time:** the person is told, and presses **Post now**. With "Post by itself" on in Setup → Rhythm, it posts on its own. 🔑
- **Post now:** posts straight away. 🔑
- **The time:** the hour our own posts did best, once there are 5 or more; until then, the evening.
- If posting fails, it says so once and does not keep retrying.

Publishing is 🔑 because it needs a public web address (`PUBLIC_BASE_URL`), which is off on purpose. With it off, approving
says what is missing and nothing goes out.

## The Plan tab

Studio → Plan is the one place for what is coming and what is waiting.

- **Waiting for you:** everything that needs a person, across every post: scripts to pick, briefs to approve, posts to publish.
- **The plan:** choose 1 week, 2 weeks or 1 month. Each day shows the post, why it was chosen and its format.
  - **Approve** keeps it on its day. **Remove** takes it off and lists it under Came off. The day stays empty until someone makes a new plan.
  - **Write it now** writes that day's three scripts straight away. They appear under Waiting for you, then Read the scripts opens them.
  - Nothing is written or paid for until a day comes or a person presses Write it now.
- **Going out:** what is scheduled and what is live.
- **Came off:** what was rejected or stopped.

Once a script is picked the other two options go away, and the chosen one is under What it made so far on the Run tab.

## The free video

A new shot about every 2.6 seconds, with moves between them, the hook as a title, captions from the script's own words in chunks of up to
six words, and an end card with the brand and the code word. Music is added when `DIWAN_FREE_MUSIC` points to a track. The voice is the
Windows one, which is the part that still sounds like a computer.

## Who chooses what to post

`agents/planner.py`: the Strategist (Muse Spark) chooses each day and says why in a plain sentence. It reads the account's stage, what brought
leads, what is winning for competitors, and what is new. The code decides what may go on a day: only real options, nothing twice, the no-repeat
rules, nothing a person rejected, and days a person approved stay as they are. If the model fails, the plain plan stands and the Plan tab says so.

- **Plan from today** first asks how far ahead (just today, this week, 2 weeks, a month) and how many posts a week, then plans only those days, spread through the week.
- **Change** and **Remove** on any planned day: a person's choice counts as approved, so a new plan keeps it.
- **Plan the next week by itself** (Setup → Rhythm, off by default) plans a week and renews it when fewer than 3 days are left. It writes nothing.
- **Run automatically** then writes each day's scripts in the morning, for a person to pick.
- A listing added on its own in the last 7 days goes first, after the Introduce post on a new account. A batch of more than 3 in a day is an import, not news.

## A video without photos

Muse makes images, not video. For a post about the brand or a topic, **Make the frames with AI** draws 6 illustrations from the approved script
(about $0.24), and **Make free video** turns them into the reel with the voice, moves and captions. They are illustrations, not photographs, with
no people, buildings or text. A listing never gets them: a drawn home would pass for the property, so a listing needs its real photos.

## Evidence and the Creative Director

Every planned day says what it is based on: your catalog, your knowledge base or company facts, plus what competitors' winning posts show (`planner.evidence`, `reader.winning`).
AI-made pictures go to the Creative Director (`agents/director.py`), which scores each out of 5 against the post, the business and competitors. Below 4 it is redrawn, up to twice. If too few pass, nothing is offered and it says why. Real photos are not held back. If the look can't be done, the frames say "not checked". A person still approves everything.

## Format and learning

Real material goes first: footage, then usable photos. A teaching post with neither becomes a graphic when at least 10 winning competitor posts lean to text on images over a person on camera (`eventpost._text_wins`); otherwise it stays to camera. A post about the brand always stays a person speaking.
It learns (`agents/taste.py`): every yes, no, change and lead updates weights on what the post was, as an online logistic regression, recent decisions counting more and a lead counting double. The weights change the choices: what goes on the plan, the format, which script comes first. After repeated noes a feature stops being chosen. A person's reason is read by the model and becomes features too. The Plan tab shows what it has learned, and any of it can be forgotten.
