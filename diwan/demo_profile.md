## Where we work

We specialise in four communities: Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC), Business Bay and Downtown Dubai. We
also handle Dubai Hills Estate for returning clients. We do not take listings outside Dubai.

## What we do

- Sales of ready apartments, townhouses and villas, for buyers and sellers.
- Off-plan advisory: we compare payment plans from several developers and explain the handover risks in plain words.
- Leasing for landlords and tenants, with yearly or monthly cheques.
- Property management for overseas owners: rent collection, maintenance and yearly inspections.
- Mortgage introductions to partner banks. We do not give financial advice and we never promise rental yields or price rises.

## How buying with us works

1. A 20-minute call to understand the budget, the area and the timeline.
2. A shortlist of up to five homes within 48 hours, each with the service charge and the permit number.
3. Viewings with one of our brokers, Monday to Saturday from 10am to 7pm. Please bring a photo ID for the building lobby.
4. An offer in writing, then Form F (the Memorandum of Understanding) signed by buyer and seller.
5. Transfer at the Dubai Land Department trustee office, usually within 30 to 45 days for a cash purchase.

## Fees

Our buyer commission is 2% of the price plus VAT. The Dubai Land Department transfer fee is 4%. The trustee office
fee and the title deed fee are paid at transfer. For leasing, the tenant pays 5% of the yearly rent plus VAT.
